# CAREX — SIH 7-to-10 Minute Judge Demonstration Script

## Presentation Structure for SIH Evaluators & Clinical Jury

---

### Step 1: Hook & Problem Statement (0:00 – 1:00)

> **Speaker:**
> *"Respected judges and jury members, modern hospitals suffer from a critical operational breakdown: **Departmental Silos**. 
> When the Emergency Department experiences a mass trauma inflow, the Inpatient Wards, Intensive Care Units, Diagnostic Labs, and Pharmacy are left unaware until a catastrophic bottleneck occurs.
> 
> Existing Hospital Information Systems (HIS) are passive databases—they store records, but they cannot predict or coordinate.
> 
> Today, we present **CAREX**, the AI-powered Hospital Management & Centralized Patient Coordination Platform with an autonomous **Hospital Brain**."*

---

### Step 2: The Flagship Hospital Brain & Explainable AI (1:00 – 3:00)

> **Action:** Navigate to **Hospital Brain** (`/admin/hospital-brain` or via Sidebar).
>
> **Speaker:**
> *"Here you see the heart of CAREX: the **Hospital Brain**. 
> Rather than bombarding administrators with disconnected alerts, the Brain correlates telemetry across all hospital units into an **Explainable 0–100 Operational Priority Score**.
> 
> Notice this active signal: *Compound Hospital Capacity Risk*. If we click **Why?**, the Explainability Modal reveals:
> 1. **Root-Cause Telemetry**: Emergency arrivals (+45%), ICU saturation (94%), and Diagnostic Lab TAT delays.
> 2. **Model Transparency**: Clearly disclosing whether the insight was generated by a *Trained Gradient-Boosted Model*, *Statistical Survival Analysis*, or *Deterministic Rule Engine*.
> 3. **Action Center Protocol**: Recommending actionable mitigations like opening overflow room 4B or auditing step-down candidates."*

### Step 3: SIH Deterministic Demo Control Center (3:00 – 5:30)

> **Action:** Navigate to **SIH Demo Center** (`/admin/demo`).
>
> **Speaker:**
> *"To demonstrate CAREX under extreme operational stress, we built an isolated **SIH Demo Simulation Center**.

> 1. *Emergency Telemetry Ingestion* classifies incoming triage acuity.
> 2. *Bed Horizon AI* projects ICU exhaustion within 4 hours.
> 3. *Queue Pacing AI* detects OPD saturation.

> 5. *Hospital Capacity Risk* generates an urgent priority alert.
> 6. *Closed-Loop Action Protocol* routes instant tasks to on-duty staff.
